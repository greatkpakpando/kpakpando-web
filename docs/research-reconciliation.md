# Research reconciliation

Reviewed on 1 November 2026. All three URLs initially returned only login-shell text in the browsing tool. A second retrieval using Node HTTPS fetch obtained HTTP 200 HTML with React Router's serialized shared-post data. The embedded `fallbackMarkdown` was decoded and the complete shared assistant responses were read, including embedded writing blocks. Each shared post contains one assistant message. None contains the client's original prompt, subsequent clarifications or acceptance messages. These are proposed drafts, not evidence of client approval.

## Consistent direction

Dignity, compassion, practical support, widow empowerment, children's welfare, girls' opportunities, youth development, prevention, responsible partnerships and no unsupported clinical claims. All responses use “Still Caring. Still Serving. Just in a Different Way.” as a proposed motto. This motto has not been confirmed by a visible client message and is not substituted for the seal's verified wording.

## Reconciliation decisions

- The official red/gold seal and supplied genuine photographs take precedence over AI-generated image queries and suggested new green branding.
- Use the latest draft's useful four-part grouping as editorial organization where it overlaps with the historical source: family welfare/livelihoods; widow dignity and rights; education and skills; drug-awareness outreach. This grouping does not assert that four fully operational, funded programmes or new professional services exist.
- Widow advocacy and protection of girls' rights are already documented in the former site. A dedicated rights page explains this purpose and offers a general contact route, without claiming an established pro bono network, accepting confidential case files or promising representation.
- Make girls' education and inclusive youth skills explicit as goals supported by the original objectives and founder history; do not advertise AI-suggested courses, dates or places.
- Substance use content focuses on the documented awareness sessions. Mental health and recovery remain a discussion/referral-development direction; no detoxification, residential rehabilitation, counselling or emergency availability is asserted.
- Keep the founder history and historical vision. Do not replace them with the unsupported 2023 date or imply aspirational facilities have been built.
- Implement useful support enquiries through direct contact. No payment flow, donation account, intake form, volunteer sign-up, unverified partner, fake impact figures or invented news is published.
- Impact is represented by documented activity photographs rather than provisional numbers. Formal reports, dated news, governance policies and donation capabilities can be added when supplied and approved.

## Missing evidence

Client prompts/clarifications/approvals for all three shared posts; founding-year confirmation; legal-service capacity, referral partners and eligibility; proposed training availability; adopted safeguarding/privacy/complaints policies; confirmed contacts; programme evidence and publication consent. No full multi-turn conversation was retrieved, although all three shared assistant responses were retrieved successfully. The website makes conservative use of the overlapping verified content while these gaps remain documented.
